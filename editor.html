Place: click the dotted playable area to add the chosen object. Select: click bricks or markers, or drag a box around them. Shift/Ctrl/⌘-click (or “Add to selection” on touch) toggles items; Shift-drag adds a box. Drag any selected marker to move the whole group. Arrow keys nudge, Delete removes events, Esc clears.

The preview shows spawn locations, not enemy movement or player pickups. With “show all” off, food, items, and weapons disappear at their expiry. Items and weapons with a blank lifetime remain until the level ends. Enemy and soccer ball spawn markers persist. Wheels snap to the nearest playable edge. Robo Goalies spawn at their markers and move toward the nearest Goal brick; without a Goal brick they immediately die. Other enemies and balls can be placed anywhere. Bricks & surfaces

Select a boundary brick to change its cooldown type.

Choose the brick whose attributes the combined group inherits:

Combine adjacent bricks, including across corners. Split restores individual sizes with the group's current attributes. Cinder combines only with Cinder; Goal only with Goal. Both are solid and cannot activate in gameplay or have modifiers or contact surfaces. Change their type to remove them.

Mechanics & reset

Changes apply immediately to selected bricks. Contact surfaces replace one another; modifiers can combine with any surface. Crumbler counts selections; Warped absorbs neighbors temporarily. Slime holds Koshi; Springs reflect him; Spikes hurt; Conveyor carries; Boost launches from rest at double speed.

Set portals one brick at a time or in groups. Each used color must have exactly two bricks before playtest or export. Warnings do not prevent editing or saving drafts. Recoloring or removing an endpoint does not change its partner.

Toggles start disabled: red 1, green 2, blue 4. Every successful activation counts, including the Toggle itself. At zero it enables; the next activation resets it. Failed clicks do not count.

Spawners glow through dark rainbow colors until physical contact spawns one item, then turn dark grey. Clicking alone does not spawn. Restart restores the charge.

Exact position

0% is the left/top of the safe playable area; 100% is the right/bottom. Use numeric coordinates for keyboard placement or precise alignment.

Several events are selected. Only fields you change are applied to all of them; untouched fields marked “Mixed” keep each event's own value. Clearing lifetime keeps selected items and weapons until the level ends (food needs a lifetime). Setting X or Y lines the whole group up on that coordinate.

Lifetimes start when an object appears. Leave item and weapon lifetimes blank to keep them until the level ends. This does not change an equipped sword's attacks or a collected power-up's duration. Food still requires a lifetime. Soccer balls are neutral, persistent kickable objects, not pickups; they have no lifetime or shield. Place Goal bricks to score.
