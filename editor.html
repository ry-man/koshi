BRICK TYPE

Select a boundary brick to change its cooldown type.

Slimed, Springs, and Spikes replace one another. Slime color is independent of brick cooldown: blue holds for 5 seconds, green for 3, red for 1, then flashes a release cue. Springs have a thin brown platform with a dark outline only on the arena-facing edge. They bounce Koshi even during cooldown; coils underneath appear only during the bounce. White triangular spikes stop Koshi and cost one heart per contact, not repeatedly while resting against them. All three work with Crumbler and Warped attributes.

Changes apply immediately to every selected brick; only the control you change is applied, so mixed values elsewhere are kept. Desktop bricks rotate 90 degrees clockwise from the phone layout; selection and attributes follow the same brick between previews. Attributes work with any cooldown color. Crumblers stay solid after their selections run out. Warped bricks absorb one random perimeter neighbor or group per selection, including around corners and during cooldown; the first absorption starts the timer. If a Warped Crumbler runs out of selections, its enlarged shape freezes until restart or absorption by another Warped brick. Playtest to see contact effects, absorption, and countdowns.
