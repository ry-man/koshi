01 / MOVEMENT

Point. Launch. Redirect.

Koshi travels to the exact point you click on a brick, stopping at the first brick he touches. Pick a new brick while moving to change course.

02 / TIMING

Give bricks a breather.

Used bricks need to recharge. Cracked Crumblers count down their remaining selections (C); at zero they stay solid but cannot be selected. Swirl-patterned Warped bricks absorb a neighbor each selection; their timer (W) counts down until all absorbed bricks return. An exhausted Warped Crumbler permanently freezes its entire enlarged shape (W:X) until restart.
