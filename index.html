01 / MOVEMENT

Point. Launch. Redirect.

Koshi travels to the exact point you click on a brick, stopping at the first brick he touches. Pick a new brick while moving to change course.

02 / TIMING

Give bricks a breather.

Bricks only activate when Koshi can move toward the clicked spot. Used bricks need to recharge. Crumblers with chipped edges count down their remaining selections (C); at zero they stay solid but cannot be selected. Swirl-patterned Warped bricks absorb a neighbor each selection, even cooling-down bricks or entire warped groups; the selected owner's timer (W) controls restoration. An exhausted Warped Crumbler freezes its enlarged shape (W:X) until restart or absorption by another Warped brick. Blue slime holds Koshi for 5 seconds, green for 3, and red for 1 until the FREE! cue. Slime color is independent of brick cooldown. Brown metallic Springs reflect him immediately, even during cooldown. White triangular Spikes stop Koshi and cost one heart per contact, without repeated damage while resting against them. Slimed, Springs, and Spikes replace one another in the editor.

03 / SNACKS & SWORDS

Snack. Heal. Fight.

Meet the food goal to win; Levels 1–3 through 1–5 also require 4, 8, and 12 robot defeats. From Level 1–2 onward, every 10 food spawns a healing heart. Pick up a sword to automatically strike robots: wood lasts 1 defeat, bronze 3, silver 10, and gold 15 seconds.
